## Which product category generates the most revenue, and how do the categories compare?

### Finding

Electronics generated the highest revenue, while Beauty generated the lowest revenue.
There was approximately a £200,000 difference in revenue between the highest- and lowest-performing categories.

### Business Interpretation

Electronics is a major contributor to overall revenue and may require continued inventory availability and marketing attention.
The lower revenue from Beauty could indicate lower customer spending or demand compared with the other categories.

## Revenue vs Quantity

## Which category sells the most products, and does the category with the highest quantity also generate the highest revenue?

### Finding

Clothing had the highest total quantity sold, while Electronics generated the highest total revenue.

### Business Interpretation

The category with the highest sales volume is not necessarily the category generating the most revenue.
Electronics appears to generate more revenue per transaction or product, while Clothing has a higher sales volume.
The business should consider both sales quantity and revenue when evaluating category performance.
